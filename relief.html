Serving Communities in Times of Crisis and Need

At Sharing Smiles Pakistan, we believe that sharing the love of Christ includes providing practical help to those in urgent need. Through humanitarian aid and disaster relief, we respond to natural disasters, pandemics, floods, and incidents of persecution.

Our approach is community-based, participatory, and compassionate, combining spiritual encouragement with practical assistance to bring hope in the most difficult circumstances.

Why Disaster Relief?

Bringing practical help when families need it most.

Disasters and crises often leave families without food, shelter, clothing, clean water, medical support, or basic household supplies. In these moments, timely support can help families survive, recover, and begin rebuilding their lives.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan responds with compassion and urgency, working through volunteers, local partners, and community leaders to identify families in need and deliver practical support.

Our relief work includes:

  • Emergency food support
  • Hygiene kits and household supplies
  • Medical camps and health support
  • Clothing and shelter support
  • Rehabilitation and rebuilding assistance

Relief History

Responding to Disasters Across Pakistan Since 2005

Oct 2005 – Sep 2007

Pakistan Earthquake Relief & Rehabilitation Project

Location: District Mansehra, NWFP Province, Pakistan

The 2005 earthquake was a major earthquake in Pakistan. It occurred on 8 October 2005 and registered a moment magnitude of 7.6. It caused 73,338 deaths and left around 3 million people homeless.

In response, Sharing Smiles Pakistan, then operating under the name PakCare, with the help of partners, distributed tents, food packs, jackets, kitchen sets, shoes, hygiene kits, clothing, water coolers, metal sheets for reconstruction, and other essential supplies.

The project also sponsored 500 school children with books, bags, uniforms, shoes, and other educational support.

Nov 2008 – Dec 2008

Baluchistan Earthquake Relief Project

Location: District Ziarat, Baluchistan Province, Pakistan

A devastating 6.4 magnitude earthquake struck Baluchistan, leaving more than 329 people dead, 1,000 injured, 17,870 houses damaged, and 133,758 individuals homeless.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan, with the help of its partners, distributed tents, food packs, blankets, hygiene kits, and metal sheets for the reconstruction of homes.

Mar 2009 – Aug 2009

Relief and Rehabilitation for War Affected IDPs

Location: District Mardan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Province, Pakistan

Armed conflict in northern Pakistan forced millions of people to leave their homes and migrate to safer areas including Swabi, Mansehra, Mardan, and Peshawar.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan assisted internally displaced families with the registration process and distributed food items, shelter materials, tents, kitchen sets, water coolers, hygiene kits, and other essential supplies.

Relief items were distributed among 685 internally displaced families in District Mardan.

Sep 2010 – Dec 2010

Pakistan Flood Response Project - Southern Punjab

Location: District Muzaffargarh, Punjab Province, Pakistan

A major breach in flood protection embankments near Taunsa Barrage caused unprecedented flooding in Kot Addu Tehsil, District Muzaffargarh. Families suffered major losses of homes, livestock, and crops.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan provided relief packages to 500 households, including shelter tents, food items, hygiene kits, water filters, and other non-food supplies.

Around 1,600 flood-affected children were also provided with milk, biscuits, and blankets.

Mar 2011 – Mar 2012

Rehabilitation: Reconstructing Flood Affected Houses

Location: District Muzaffargarh, Southern Punjab, Pakistan

Sharing Smiles Pakistan initiated a rehabilitation project to help flood-affected families recover from the devastation and rebuild their lives.

The project supported families whose homes and community infrastructure were fully or partially damaged by the floods.

Overall, 118 affected families received support for rebuilding houses and restoring community infrastructure.

Aug 2022 – Dec 2022

Nowshera Relief Project

Location: District Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Province, Pakistan

Local crises and flooding in Nowshera left many families struggling with shortages of food, clean water, and essential household supplies.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan, in collaboration with local partners and community leaders, provided emergency food supplies, hygiene kits, and household essentials to vulnerable families.

Aug 2023 – Dec 2023

Jaranwala Emergency Response Project

Location: Jaranwala, Faisalabad District, Punjab Province, Pakistan

In August 2023, Christian communities in Jaranwala experienced a tragic wave of violence that resulted in the destruction and vandalism of churches, homes, and personal property.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan mobilized emergency relief assistance, providing food supplies, shelter support, household essentials, and humanitarian care to affected families.

Jul 2025 – Oct 2025

Jhang & Chiniot Flood Relief Project

Location: District Jhang and District Chiniot, Punjab Province, Pakistan

Severe flooding in Jhang and Chiniot caused widespread devastation, leaving many families homeless and without access to food, clean drinking water, healthcare services, and other basic necessities.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan conducted rapid needs assessments and launched an emergency relief response. Activities included emergency food packages, cooked meals, clothing, hygiene supplies, and medical assistance.

A medical camp was organized that provided healthcare services to more than 1,300 individuals in flood-affected communities.
