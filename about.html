ABOUT SHARING SMILES PAKISTAN

Proclaiming Christ and serving communities across Pakistan since 2004.

WHO WE ARE

Serving Pakistan through Word and Deed.

We believe true transformation happens when people encounter both the Gospel of Jesus Christ and the practical love of God demonstrated through compassionate service.

For this reason, our ministry combines church planting, evangelism, leadership development, Bible distribution, education, vocational training, medical support, clean water initiatives, and humanitarian relief to reach vulnerable and unreached communities across Pakistan.

OUR TEAM

United with one vision for the Gospel.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan is powered by pastors, missionaries, evangelists, volunteers, and ministry workers who faithfully serve communities across Pakistan with dedication, compassion, and a shared passion for Christ.

WHY PAKISTAN?

A nation with great spiritual need.

Pakistan is home to more than 220 million people and stands at the heart of the 10/40 Window — one of the most spiritually unreached regions in the world. As the second-largest Islamic nation, nearly 97% of the population is Muslim, while Christians make up only a small minority. Millions across the nation have little or no access to the Gospel, strong local churches, trained leaders, or discipleship resources. Many believers face religious discrimination, persecution, economic hardship, and social rejection, yet the need for the hope of Christ continues to grow every day.

Sharing Smiles Pakistan exists to bring the love and message of Jesus Christ to unreached communities through evangelism, church planting, Bible distribution, leadership training, discipleship, and compassionate outreach. Across villages, towns, and cities, lives are being transformed by the Gospel, but the growing Church still faces tremendous challenges. Many newly planted churches gather in homes, open fields, or temporary shelters because they have no place of worship. There is also a severe shortage of Bibles, Gospel literature, indigenous missionaries, and faith-based training materials needed to strengthen new believers in their walk with Christ.

Strategically located between Afghanistan, Iran, China, and India, Pakistan holds incredible importance for global missions. More than half of the population has never personally heard the message of Jesus Christ, making the mission both urgent and vital. Through the grace of God and the partnership of faithful supporters around the world, Sharing Smiles Pakistan is committed to reaching the lost, equipping believers, strengthening the local Church, and bringing hope to communities across the nation — one soul at a time.
